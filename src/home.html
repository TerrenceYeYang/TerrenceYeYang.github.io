03 · About

About Me

I split my time between applied AI research and hands-on engineering — roughly half academic, half production. My work centers on retrieval and agent systems that need to be trustworthy in domains where a confident wrong answer is costly: banking, compliance, and technical documentation.

Alongside that, I build tools for measuring and understanding model behavior — local benchmark dashboards, evidence repositories, and small community products — because good research needs good instruments.
